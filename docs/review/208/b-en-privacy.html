Privacy policy

Your data, optional ads and your privacy choices.

Last updated: 2026-10-05

Dimension Riders is an Android game made by Murilo Toloni.

What the game collects

The game itself does not collect, store or send any personal data. It has no accounts and no analytics of its own, and it works without an internet connection.

Ads

The game shows video ads only when you choose to watch one (for example, to double the Amber earned at the end of a run). The game does not show automatic ads. You may also choose a rewarded video for a second chance after defeat.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect from your device:

  • the advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • the IP address, from which an approximate location is derived;
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data (crashes and performance).

This data goes to Google and its partners, not to the developer of the game. How Google uses it: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Don't watch: ads are optional and the whole game works without them. With no internet connection, no ad is loaded.
  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example, in Europe and the United Kingdom), the game asks before using your data for personalised ads. You can review your answer in Settings, inside the game.
  • Advertising ID: in Android settings (Privacy > Ads) you can reset or delete your advertising ID.

What stays on your device

Your progress (runs, best times, Amber and what you have unlocked) is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the game erases it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Google Play

If you install the game from Google Play, Google may process data under its own privacy policy, which is independent of this game.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting any other data (for example, an online leaderboard), this page will say exactly what, why and how to opt out, before that version is released.

Contact

Questions: support@dimensionriders.app.
