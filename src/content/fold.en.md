# The Fold

No one knows who folded the worlds. No one knows if anyone did.

## Layers

The worlds lie in layers, one over the other, like folded paper. Where the Fold sits flat, each world lives without knowing about the rest. That is balance. Time runs at a different pace in every layer: where it runs slow it is still the stone age, where it runs fast there are already machines. That is why the creeps come from every era and every kind of creature.

## The Amber

In the creases, where two layers press hardest against each other, the rubbing of worlds leaves a residue. It hardens slowly, in golden veins nobody can explain. The Riders call it Amber. Like the amber you know, it keeps what got caught in it: a fossil of two worlds at once.

On its own, a vein of Amber only glows. But when a lot of Amber gathers in one place, or when someone heats it on purpose, the two layers caught in it remember each other and touch. That is a Tear. And the longer a Tear stays open, the wider it gets.

## The Mark

Every vein of Amber, in every crease, in every layer, keeps one more impression besides the two worlds that made it. Always the same one. It belongs to no known world. The Riders call it the Mark. Nobody can read it. Nobody knows if it is a signature, a scar or a warning.

## The Unfolded

The first to learn what Amber does were the ones with a reason to run: the defeated, the banished, the ones who ruined their own world. They gather Amber into nests, open a Tear, and cross into a layer where nobody is ready for them. They say the Mark proves the worlds were once one, and that the Fold is a prison built by whoever pulled them apart. Unfolding is freedom, they say. And whoever frees the worlds first gets to rule them.

One of the Unfolded strong enough to hold a Tear open and command both sides of it is a Tyrant.

## The Riders

An old order, from many worlds. Every world that joined remembers the day a Tyrant arrived. The order has a single rule, older than any theory: an open Tear is a Tear to seal. The Riders say nothing about the Mark. Each of them has an answer. None of them tells it.

Every Rider carries a Shard: one fragment of Amber, chipped and polished. A Shard opens nothing. It only lets one person through a Tear that already exists. And it pulls. When a Tear is sealed, the Fold snaps shut, and the Shard drags its Rider to the next crease that is giving way. Riders do not choose where they go. They ride the pull.

Both sides carry Amber. Only one of them wants to open.
