# Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-05

Dimension Riders is a game for Android made by Murilo Toloni.

## What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not collect, store or send any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party services. It does not need an internet connection to play.

## What stays on your device

Your progress (levels cleared and best times) is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the game deletes it. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Google Play

If you install the game from Google Play, Google may process data under its own privacy policy, which is independent of this game.

## Changes

If the game ever starts collecting data (for example, an online leaderboard), this page will say exactly what, why, and how to opt out, before that version is released.
