Dimension Riders is an Android game made in Brazil. The game's developer is responsible for the data described here. Contact: [support@dimensionriders.app](mailto:support@dimensionriders.app).

You can play without a linked account. Progress stays on your device. On Android, Play Games may recognize your gamer profile; in Settings you can also link a Google Account to recover your progress. These sign-in methods can access the same game account. See the available copies under "Copies of your progress".

## What leaves your device

Google Analytics for Firebase (metrics): how the game is played (runs, sessions, performance), the version, the device, the language, the approximate country from the IP address and an installation identifier, without the advertising ID; used to tune the difficulty.

Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports): when the game crashes, the error, the code location, the version, the device and installation and session identifiers; used to fix errors.

Google AdMob (optional rewarded videos): to show and measure ads and prevent fraud, it may process the advertising ID, other device identifiers, your IP address and your interactions with ads; the ad kit starts when the game opens. More: [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Random identifier

To record daily rewards and never pay the same one twice, the game creates on its own, in Firebase, a random identifier with no name or email, holding the receipts and a day counter. Linking an account makes this identifier usable for progress recovery. Google and Firebase may process identity data authorized during sign-in, such as your name and email; the game does not request access to Gmail messages.

## Copies of your progress

Android backup: with Google backup turned on in your phone (in the Android settings), the game's progress file goes with it to your Google account and comes back when you reinstall the game or change phones. Only the progress goes (Amber, items, talents, records); the run in progress, the settings and the privacy choices stay on the device. Turning the Android backup off prevents the copy.

Play Games: if you use Play Games on your phone, the game signs in with it on its own when it opens and links the game account to your gamer profile, to recover your progress when you reinstall the game or change phones.

Firebase game account: for linked accounts, progress is synchronized at moments such as opening the game, run end, account linking, purchases, forging and menu changes. A compressed snapshot is stored in Firestore under the game account identifier. Synchronization uses an available connection, preserves this device's run and asks for confirmation before merging this device's progress into another account.

## Permission, and how to turn it off

In the European Union, the European Economic Area, Switzerland and the United Kingdom, metrics and crash reports only leave with your permission, asked the first time you open the game; outside those regions, sending starts on, based on our legitimate interest in improving the game. Turn each one on or off in Settings > Terms and privacy > Data options. Personalised ads: in the European region, Google asks first (change it in Settings > Ad privacy); elsewhere, there is no personalisation.

## How long we keep data

Some records are subject to cleanup after 60 days. Linked accounts and accounts with purchases are protected from this cleanup; linked account progress remains until account deletion. This does not mean all metrics, advertising data or backups have the same retention period. Google, our technology partner, has its own retention periods and may keep data longer to provide its services, prevent fraud or meet legal obligations. Learn more: [policies.google.com/technologies/retention](https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention).

## Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to Google (Firebase, AdMob, Android backup and Google Play Games), on servers that may be outside Brazil. Processing follows each service's terms; advertising may involve Google's partners. We don't sell data.

## Children and teenagers

The game is not directed at children under 13; in the European regions above, anyone under 16 should ask a parent or guardian before allowing sending.

## Your rights

In the app, Settings > Account > Delete account lets you request deletion of your game account and its associated Firebase progress; a connection is needed for the request to reach the server. Deletion applies to the game account open on the device; for another account of yours (for example, one from another Play Games profile that was merged into this one), contact support. The Android backup and Play Games data are managed separately in your Google account. Deleting the game account does not automatically delete metrics or advertising data held by those services. Turning metrics off or uninstalling does not erase data already sent. For questions and requests about your rights, contact [support@dimensionriders.app](mailto:support@dimensionriders.app). You can also contact Brazil's ANPD or your country's data protection authority.

In-app requests require a connection and are processed by the daily cleanup job. Without the app, email [support@dimensionriders.app](mailto:support@dimensionriders.app) with the subject ACCOUNT DELETION - DIMENSION RIDERS and the email of your linked Google Account or your Play Games gamer name. Ownership must be verified; do not send passwords or login codes. An account without a linked Google Account or Play Games profile may not be identifiable after uninstalling.

## This website

The dimensionriders.app website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub records visitors' IP address and browser in its security logs.

To learn how people find the game, the website uses Google Analytics: the page visited, the site or campaign you came from, the language, the device type, the approximate country and whether you tapped the Google Play button. To count visitors, it stores the _ga cookies, which recognize the same browser for up to 60 days after the last visit, without linking the visit to the game. This data is not used for ads. More: [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

In the European Union, the European Economic Area, Switzerland and the United Kingdom, the cookies are stored only if you accept them in the website's notice; without that, Google receives only a record of the visit, with no cookie. Your choice is kept in the browser, and the Cookies button in the footer reopens the notice. Outside these regions, counting with cookies starts on, based on our legitimate interest in understanding how the game is promoted; to opt out, delete or block the website's cookies in your browser or use a content blocker.

## Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the new version goes into the game and onto the dimensionriders.app website, where the date at the top of the page shows the latest update.
