## Contact us

Email [support@dimensionriders.app](mailto:support@dimensionriders.app), in English or Portuguese. We reply within a week.

To help us find the problem faster, tell us:

- your phone model and Android version;
- the game version, shown at the bottom of Settings (for example, v0.1.0);
- if the problem is about the account or the progress, the account's support code (Settings > Account > Support code; one tap copies it);
- what you were doing when it happened and, if you can, a screenshot or a short video.

## FAQ

### I changed phones or reinstalled the game. Can I get my progress back?

Yes, if there was a copy. With Google backup turned on in the Android settings, your progress comes back on its own when you reinstall the game or set up the new phone with the same Google account. With a linked account, progress is also kept in the game's cloud: with the same Play Games profile, it comes back on its own when you open the game; if it doesn't, tap Settings > Account > Game account (Google) and sign in with the linked Google Account. Without a recoverable copy, progress is lost when you change phones, uninstall the game or clear its data.

### The ad won't load

Ads need an internet connection, and one isn't always available. Try again later. The whole game works without them.

### The game closed by itself or froze

Update the game on Google Play and restart your phone. If it keeps happening, email support with the details above.

### Does the game have purchases? Can I get a refund?

The game is free and sells nothing. Amber is earned by playing and can't be bought.

### How do I delete my data?

In the game, Settings > Account > Delete account closes the account immediately and requests deletion of the associated Firebase progress, erased by the daily cleanup job by the next day (if the account for the saved progress is no longer available on the device, the button lets you erase only this device's progress). A connection is required. Without the app, visit https://dimension-riders.web.app/excluir-conta. If you use Play Games, sign in with the Google Account used for that profile and authorize identification through Play Games. You do not need to reinstall the game or have saved your support code. After your account is identified, confirm the deletion request on the page; support carries out the deletion after checking the request. The request applies only to the account identified through Play Games. If you have another game account linked separately to your Google Account, it may be a different account and will not be deleted automatically along with it. To request deletion of that other account, email support@dimensionriders.app from its linked email address; we must confirm control of the account before acting. The support code helps locate an account, but does not prove ownership and, on its own, does not authorize deletion or disclosure of data. Never send your password or sign-in codes to support. If you cannot complete identification, contact support for review; we do not promise to locate an account using only a public player name. The Android backup, Play Games data, metrics and ads have separate controls. Linked accounts (Google Account or Play Games) and accounts with purchases do not expire under the 60-day cleanup.
