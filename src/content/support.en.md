## Contact us

Email [support@dimensionriders.app](mailto:support@dimensionriders.app), in English or Portuguese. We reply within a week.

To help us find the problem faster, tell us:

- your phone model and Android version;
- the game version, shown at the bottom of Settings (for example, v0.1.0);
- what you were doing when it happened and, if you can, a screenshot or a short video.

## FAQ

### I changed phones or reinstalled the game. Can I get my progress back?

Yes, if there was a copy. With Google backup turned on in the Android settings, your progress comes back on its own when you reinstall the game or set up the new phone with the same Google account. If you turned on Settings > Google Play backup in the game, turn it on again on the new phone, sign in with the same Play Games account and tap Restore. Without either copy, progress is lost when you change phones, uninstall the game or clear its data.

### The ad won't load

Ads need an internet connection, and one isn't always available. Try again later. The whole game works without them.

### The game closed by itself or froze

Update the game on Google Play and restart your phone. If it keeps happening, email support with the details above.

### Does the game have purchases? Can I get a refund?

The game is free and sells nothing. Amber is earned by playing and can't be bought.

### How do I delete my data?

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it erases local progress. We do not offer individual deletion of online data on request. Our retention goal is 60 days, but cleanup does not yet cover all records and Google may keep them longer, as described in the Privacy Policy. Metrics and crash reports can be turned off in Data options. Questions and rights: [support@dimensionriders.app](mailto:support@dimensionriders.app).
