## Contact us

Email [support@dimensionriders.app](mailto:support@dimensionriders.app), in English or Portuguese. We reply within a week.

To help us find the problem faster, tell us:

- your phone model and Android version;
- the game version, shown at the bottom of Settings (for example, v0.1.0);
- what you were doing when it happened and, if you can, a screenshot or a short video.

## FAQ

### I changed phones or reinstalled the game. Can I get my progress back?

Yes, if there was a copy. With Google backup turned on in the Android settings, your progress comes back on its own when you reinstall the game or set up the new phone with the same Google account. With a linked account, progress is also kept in the game's cloud: with the same Play Games profile, it comes back on its own when you open the game; if it doesn't, tap Settings > Account > Game account (Google) and sign in with the linked Google Account. Without a recoverable copy, progress is lost when you change phones, uninstall the game or clear its data.

### The ad won't load

Ads need an internet connection, and one isn't always available. Try again later. The whole game works without them.

### The game closed by itself or froze

Update the game on Google Play and restart your phone. If it keeps happening, email support with the details above.

### Does the game have purchases? Can I get a refund?

The game is free and sells nothing. Amber is earned by playing and can't be bought.

### How do I delete my data?

In the game, Settings > Account > Delete account requests deletion of the account and its associated Firebase progress (if the account for the saved progress is no longer available on the device, the button lets you erase only this device's progress). A connection is required; the daily cleanup job processes the request. Without the app, email [support@dimensionriders.app](mailto:support@dimensionriders.app) with the subject ACCOUNT DELETION - DIMENSION RIDERS and the email of your linked Google Account or your Play Games gamer name. We verify ownership before deleting data; do not send passwords or login codes. An account without a linked Google Account or Play Games profile may not be identifiable after uninstalling. The Android backup, Play Games data, metrics and ads have separate controls. Linked accounts and accounts with purchases do not expire under the 60-day cleanup.
