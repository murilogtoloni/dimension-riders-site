## Contact us

Email [support@dimensionriders.app](mailto:support@dimensionriders.app), in English or Portuguese. We reply within a week.

To help us find the problem faster, tell us:

- your phone model and Android version;
- the game version, shown at the bottom of Settings (for example, v0.1.0);
- if the problem is about the account or the progress, the account's support code (Settings > Account > Support code; one tap copies it);
- what you were doing when it happened and, if you can, a screenshot or a short video.

## FAQ

### I changed phones or reinstalled the game. Can I get my progress back?

Yes, if there was a copy. With Google backup turned on in the Android settings, your progress comes back on its own when you reinstall the game or set up the new phone with the same Google account. With a linked account, progress is also kept in the game's cloud: with the same Play Games profile, it comes back on its own when you open the game; if it doesn't, tap Settings > Account > Game account (Google) and sign in with the linked Google Account. Without a recoverable copy, progress is lost when you change phones, uninstall the game or clear its data.

### The ad won't load

Ads need an internet connection, and one isn't always available. Try again later. The whole game works without them.

### The game closed by itself or froze

Update the game on Google Play and restart your phone. If it keeps happening, email support with the details above.

### Does the game have purchases? Can I get a refund?

The game is free and sells nothing. Amber is earned by playing and can't be bought.

### How do I delete my data?

In the game, Settings > Account > Delete account (connection required): the account is closed immediately and cloud data is erased by the next day (if the account is unavailable on this device, the button erases only this device's progress). Without the app, visit https://dimension-riders.web.app/excluir-conta: if you use Play Games, sign in with that Google Account, authorize identification through Play Games and confirm the deletion request on the page (no need to reinstall or have your Support code). This request applies only to the Play Games account; another account linked separately to your Google Account will not be deleted automatically (email [support@dimensionriders.app](mailto:support@dimensionriders.app) from that Google Account). The Support code helps locate an account, but does not prove ownership, and the server does not store your gamer name. Deleting the account does not immediately erase metrics and crash reports already sent (which have no name or email); they expire under Google's retention periods. Turning metrics off or uninstalling the game does not erase data already sent either. Android backup and Play Games are managed in your Google Account, and ads in your Android ad settings. Linked accounts and accounts with purchases do not expire under the 60-day cleanup.
